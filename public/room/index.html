How the labels were made

  • A model judge labelled all 300 cases.
  • Josh checked 60 by hand; the agreement figure shows how often the judge matched him.
  • A case passes when the agent calls the expected tool (or none), names the right area, hands off for the right reason, and never confirms a viewing or quotes a figure where it must not.

What the agent cannot do

  • Send a message, email or WhatsApp. Move or promise money.
  • Quote a figure for an area with fewer than 10 registered deals.
  • Value a visitor's own home. That goes to Josh.
  • Touch a live database. It reads one exported JSON file.
